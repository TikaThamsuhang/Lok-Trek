Annapurna Base Camp Trek

An unforgettable adventure in the heart of the Himalayas

The Annapurna Base Camp Trek is an unforgettable adventure in the heart of the Himalayas. In about 7 days, you pass through terraced rice fields, traditional villages, and beautiful rhododendron forests before reaching the spectacular Annapurna Base Camp at 4,130 meters.

Each stage of the trek offers breathtaking panoramic views of Annapurna, Machhapuchhre, and Dhaulagiri, combining accessible hiking trails with some more challenging sections. Along the way, you will discover the authentic lifestyle of Nepalese mountain villages and spend your nights in comfortable tea house lodges, while enjoying the wild nature and majestic Himalayan scenery. The return journey to Pokhara includes a relaxing stop at the natural hot springs of Jhinu Danda, perfect for recovering after the trekking adventure.

Day by Day Itinerary

Trek Itinerary

Day 1

Pokhara to Sinuwa (via Jhinu Danda and Chhomrong)

The trek begins from Pokhara and continues toward Sinuwa, passing through Jhinu Danda and Chhomrong. Along the way, you will enjoy beautiful views of Machhapuchhre (6,993 m). This sacred mountain has never been climbed, as climbing it is officially forbidden.

3-4 hrs 3,200 m Lodge
Day 2

Sinuwa to Deurali

Today's trek takes you through beautiful forest trails and mountain landscapes as you continue toward Deurali, gradually gaining altitude.

5-6 hrs 3,200 m Lodge
Day 3

Deurali to Annapurna Base Camp

You trek through the Annapurna Sanctuary, surrounded by towering Himalayan peaks, before reaching Annapurna Base Camp. Enjoy the spectacular sunset views from ABC.

5-6 hrs 4,130 m Lodge
Day 4

Sunrise at ABC and Trek to Bamboo

Wake up early to witness a breathtaking sunrise panorama over the Himalayas. From ABC you can enjoy close views of Annapurna South, Annapurna I, Machhapuchhre, Himchuli, Gangapurna, Annapurna III, and many other surrounding peaks. ABC is one of the most beautiful places to watch the sunrise in the Himalayas. After breakfast, you begin the descent to Bamboo.

6-7 hrs 2,310 m Lodge
Day 5

Bamboo to Jhinu Danda

Today you trek down to Jhinu Danda, where you can relax in the natural hot springs, a perfect way to soothe your muscles after several days of trekking.

5-6 hrs 1,780 m Lodge
Day 6

Return to Pokhara

Return to Pokhara by shared jeep, traveling together with other trekkers.
